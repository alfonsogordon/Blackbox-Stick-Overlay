Custom image requirements

Formats: PNG or WebP recommended (transparent background). JPEG and SVG also accepted by the uploader.

Recommended size: 256 × 256 px for pointer and trail stamps; 512 × 512 px for stick backgrounds and crosshairs. Square (1:1) images work best.

Maximum file size: 5 MB per image. There is no fixed pixel-resolution requirement; artwork scales to fit.

Transparent images are best for overlays. Large images may slow rendering, especially with high-density trails. Images stay in your browser.

Privacy: Uploaded artwork is decoded and used in your current browser session only. It is not sent to our server, saved to an account, or added to the GitHub repository. Refreshing or closing the page clears the uploaded artwork; you will need to select it again.

Artwork is drawn locally. Choose Custom image in the matching shape/style dropdown for pointer, trails or crosshair. Background artwork overlays the selected background colour.
Image privacy: Your custom images are processed only in this browser session. They are not uploaded to a server, stored in an account, or retained by this website. Refreshing or closing the page clears them.
Transitions
Fades do not change video length. They adjust transparency within the existing exported timeline: fade-in starts at the beginning of the export, and fade-out ends at the end. If you want the sticks to fade in before flight movement starts, set lead-in at least as long as fade-in (likewise for lead-out/fade-out).
Display Other Log Info
Choose telemetry values found in the CSV, customise each colour and size, and drag enabled values around the preview. Fields depend on the log and enabled sensors. Drag enabled readouts directly on the preview. BBL and CSV fields are detected automatically.
Import a CSV to browse its logged fields.
Betaflight Log Settings
Optimised for stick overlays on small flash chips. This preset preserves the four rcCommand channels and disables optional logging data. Not suitable for PID tuning, Gyroflow or fault diagnosis.


Compatibility: Betaflight builds and targets can omit optional logging fields. First run get blackbox in CLI. If a pasted command reports INVALID NAME, omit that line. Check all changes before entering save; the generated commands intentionally do not include it. The preset does not alter your PID/filter tune.


At 1/16 sampling, log size depends on PID loop frequency and compression. An 8 MiB flash chip would hold approximately 70 minutes at 2 KiB/s, 35 minutes at 4 KiB/s or 17 minutes at 8 KiB/s, excluding overhead. These are illustrative, not measured flight times.


Privacy: imported CSV data stays in your browser. No uploads. Video export depends on browser codec support; transparency is not guaranteed in every browser.
